Project Sections by User Roles

- Admin Sections
Profile: The admin can view their personal details, information linked to the employee table, and their account login credentials. They can also change their password here.

Customers: A management area where the admin can create new customer profiles, view the list of all clients, search for users, edit client details, or delete accounts.

Employees: An HR management tool to register new bank staff, view the entire bank roster, update employee details, or remove them from the system.

Accounts: Allows the admin to open new accounts for existing customers (e.g., with different currencies or types) and view all active bank accounts.

Cards: The admin can issue and link multiple debit or credit cards to a customer’s account, view card data, or delete/cancel cards.

Transactions: The admin's manual operational ledger. They can make deposits (add cash), process withdrawals (cash-out), or log payments for customer utilities. They can also view a master table of all historical transactions.

Transfers: Used to route money internally (within our bank) or externally (to third-party banks) via card or phone numbers.

Branches: A system to manage physical brick-and-mortar bank locations, allowing the admin to create new branches, update branch info, or delete them.

Branch Expenses: A log to add and monitor operational branch overhead expenses (like rent and utility bills) paid for by the bank.

Bank Money: The bank's financial dashboard. The admin can monitor core capital funds in different currencies and review daily automated summaries tracking overall balances, expenses, and earned commissions.

Logins & Passwords: Technical security tables. "Logins" displays user IDs and assigned roles (without displaying actual passwords). "Passwords" allows the admin to view password data blocks and modify them if necessary.

Saving Deposits: Allows the admin to set up new saving deposits for clients and view a registry of all active deposits.

Central Bank: An exclusive interface where the admin can view macro-level accounts and customer data synced with the Central Bank network.

- Customer Sections
Account Selection: The initial dashboard screen. Since a customer can hold multiple accounts (e.g., Ruble account, Dollar account), they choose which specific account workspace they want to open.

Profile: The customer can view their personal information and update their security login password.

Home: The core dashboard for the selected account. The user can view their current balance, see their active cards, and track their recent account transaction history (transfers, deposits, etc.). They can also switch to their other accounts from here.

Transactions: Includes a live exchange rate price table. Here, the customer can transfer funds between their own cards, exchange currencies, and directly pay for daily bills like university tuition, electricity, and water.

Transfers: The peer-to-peer payment hub. The client can initiate internal transfers (to customers within our bank) or external transfers (to other banks) using either a card number or a phone number.

Saving Deposit: A self-service wealth management tool. The customer can open a new savings deposit by picking a lock-up period and investment amount. They can track their active deposits and manually withdraw the funds once the maturity time expires.
